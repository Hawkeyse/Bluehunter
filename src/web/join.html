What BlueHunter does for your group

Everything below is controlled by your group's admins. Turn each feature on or off from the dashboard.

🛡️Keeps it clean

Removes links, spam, flooding and forwarded messages, and locks the group if a raid starts. You choose what happens to rule-breakers: delete, warn, strike or kick.

Block linksBlock spamBlock floodingRaid lockdownBad word filterAI moderation

📈Shows who's active

A live graph of your group's messages, a top-members board, and a list of quiet members you can remove in one go.

Live activity graphTop chattersInactive members

🗑️Catches deleted messages

When someone deletes a message for everyone, admins can still see what it said and who sent it.

Deleted message logReveal view-once media

👋Runs on a schedule

Welcomes new members, says goodbye, posts good morning to good night messages, and closes and opens the chat at times you pick.

🤖Chats and plays

Replies when mentioned, and brings word games to the group.
